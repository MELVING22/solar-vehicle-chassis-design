# Solar Electric Vehicle – Chassis Design (BSVC 2024)

This repository contains my design work for the **Bharath Solar Vehicle Competition (BSVC) 2024**, where I served as the **3D Design Head** for our college’s solar-powered electric vehicle team.

The project focuses on developing a lightweight, ergonomic, and efficient chassis optimized for solar and battery-powered mobility.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

- **Project Role**: 3D Design Head  
- **Tools Used**: SolidWorks 2022, PDF Technical Drawings, Assembly Files (.SLDASM), Rendered Views  
- **Design Duration**: March 2024 – October 2024  
- **Team Outcome**: Runner-up at BSVC 2024

---

## 🧰 Key Features of the Design

- **Chassis Type**: Tubular space frame (lightweight & strong)
- **Power System**: Solar + Battery hybrid
- **Design Focus Areas**:
  - Structural safety and weight optimization
  - Driver ergonomics
  - Aerodynamics & panel fitting for solar exposure
- **Suspension and Steering**: Suspension and pedal layouts integrated
- **Modularity**: Easy to manufacture and service
